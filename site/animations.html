How to use one

  1. Download it with the button under the animation. It's a small .json file holding only colours and times, never code.
  2. In RaptorSense, open the Animations tab and press Import. Pick the file. It never replaces one you already have.
  3. Press Play on the keyboard, or pick Your animation on the Lighting tab to keep it on. Change it as much as you like.

Made for the Predator Helios 16's per-key keyboard. On the Nitro's four zones, each zone shows the average of its keys.

Share yours

In the Animations tab, pick your animation and press Export. Then fill in the short form on GitHub and drop the file in. Once it's approved it appears here, with your name on it, and you get a reply with the link. Animations that flash more than three times a second can't go up, for people who are sensitive to flashing.
