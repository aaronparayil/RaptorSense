Animations, made by people

Everything here was painted frame by frame in RaptorSense's Animations tab and shared by whoever made it. Each one plays below exactly as it would on a Predator Helios 16 keyboard.

Add to RaptorSense opens the app and adds the animation in one click. It needs RaptorSense 1.8.9 or newer, started at least once. On an older version, use Download and then Import in the Animations tab.

Nothing matches that.

How to use one

  1. Press Add to RaptorSense under the animation and let your browser open RaptorSense. It's added and starts playing on your keyboard.
  2. Or browse from inside the app: the Shared button in the Animations tab lists everything on this page, with an Add button for each.
  3. Keep it on by picking Your animation on the Lighting tab, and change it as much as you like. An animation holds only colours and times, never code, and never replaces one you already have.

Made for the Predator Helios 16's per-key keyboard. On the Nitro's four zones, each zone shows the average of its keys.

Share yours

In the Animations tab, pick your animation and press Export. Then fill in the short form on GitHub and drop the file in. It's checked automatically and, if all is well, appears here within a few minutes with your name on it, and you get a reply with the link. Animations that flash more than three times a second can't go up, for people who are sensitive to flashing.
