Your Predator's keyboard, the way you want it

RaptorSense is a free alternative to PredatorSense and NitroSense. 147 lighting effects, animations you paint yourself, lighting that follows your music and your typing, plus fans and power modes. Built by a student who found PredatorSense too limited.

Everything PredatorSense should have had

One small app in the tray. It reads your laptop's model when it starts and shows only what works on it.

147 effects, all live

Waves, fire, rain, radar, plasma, falling blocks and many more, in a gallery where every effect plays at once on its own little keyboard. One click puts it on the real one.

Your own animations

Paint frames key by key, set how long each stays and fades, and loop it. Start from a ready-made one, undo freely, and share it as a file.

Music and typing

14 effects move with whatever your PC is playing, read from the sound output, never the microphone. 23 more ripple, spark and burst from the keys you press.

Fans and power

Power profiles, fans on Auto, Max or speeds you set, live temperatures, the rear light bar, and battery health. Only changed on models where they've been tested.

Automations

When a game opens, on battery, while music plays or when you step away: switch the lighting, power mode and fans, and put them back afterwards.

The keyboard's own keys

The PREDATOR key opens RaptorSense, and Fn+F11 and Fn+F12 keep dimming and brightening the keys. In English, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese or Hindi.

Will it work on my laptop?

One download covers both tested laptops. On other models RaptorSense is careful: it lights only keyboards it knows, and it reads fans and power without changing them until that model has been checked.

Another Predator?

Per-key lighting works on keyboards 04F2:0117, 011A and 0119. The laptop report tells you which keyboard you have with one command, and it's how new models get added.

On Linux?

A preview lights the Helios's per-key keyboard, but it hasn't driven a real keyboard yet. If you run Linux on a Predator, testing it helps a lot.

Made an animation? Share it.

The animations page plays everything people have shared, right in your browser. Download one and import it in the Animations tab, or export yours and put it up for everyone.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The app isn't code-signed (that costs money every year), so Windows warns about it the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release lists the zip's SHA-256 so you can check your download, and all the code is on GitHub to read or build yourself.

Do I need to close PredatorSense?

Yes. Both try to drive the keyboard, so close PredatorSense (or NitroSense) before you start RaptorSense.

Does it send anything anywhere?

No. There are no accounts and no telemetry. Typing effects learn only which key position was pressed, never the characters. Music effects read your PC's sound output, never the microphone, and only while such an effect is in use.

Does it need administrator rights?

Not for the keyboard on the Helios. Fans, power modes and the light bar go through a small helper you install from the Performance tab, which asks once. On the Nitro, the keyboard goes through that helper too.

How do I install, update or remove it?

Extract the zip to a folder you'll keep and run RaptorSense.exe. To update, quit it from the tray and extract the new zip over the old folder; your profiles and animations stay. To remove it, run Remove fan control.cmd if you installed fan control, then delete the folder.

Is this made by Acer?

No. It's an unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Acer.
